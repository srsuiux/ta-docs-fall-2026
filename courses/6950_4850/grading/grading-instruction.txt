Grading Guidelines

Marks
The assignment is graded out of 30 marks.

How to grade

Read the assignment carefully and check that each student’s work is accurate against what was asked.
Don’t take marks off for minor syntax issues, commas, small typos, or basic slips. Leave them as they are.
Focus on whether the work is correct and complete.

How to write the feedback
Keep it clean and detailed, but generic. Don’t make it too specific to one student.
Say what the student did well (for example, “Good work on the routing setup”).
Say what is missing, if anything.
Say what can be improved, if anything.
If there is nothing missing or nothing to improve, don’t mention those parts.

Tone
Make it sound natural and simple.
Use plain words. Avoid heavy or fancy language.
