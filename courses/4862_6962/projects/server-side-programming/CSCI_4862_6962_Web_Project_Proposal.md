## Introduction

Throughout this semester you will be developing a **server-side web site** for selling **books** by some **specific author**.

To make sure everyone gets to create something different, each project in the class must cover a **different author**. Authors are **first come, first served**. If you want a particular author, submit your proposal as soon as possible to reserve them.

To keep things simple, your site will sell **three specific books** by that author. For each book, you will need:

- The **title** of the book.
- The **price** of the book.
- The **ISBN** of the book.
- An **image** of the book cover.

The Amazon website is a good place to find all of this information.

## What to Turn In

The proposal is due **Wednesday, September 10**. However, you may submit it **as soon as possible** if there is a specific author you would like to reserve.

Your proposal should include:

- The name of the author.
- The three books by that author you intend to sell, including the title, price, and ISBN for each.

You are to submit it to the **Blackboard assignment link** this document was attached to.

After you submit your proposal, please **check for any replies** to your submission. This may be either:

- An **approval** for your project. At that point, you will receive the full **5/5 points** for the proposal.
- A notification that your author has **already been reserved** by another student, in which case you will need to choose another author. The instructor will list authors already reserved on the Blackboard site, so be sure to check that before submitting your proposal.
