# Study Log

Sundar Raj · how the proposal changed over time. The current content is in `study-document-nov-3.md`. This log only records what changed and why, so nothing is repeated.

## Oct 7: first draft

- Conditions were A (student plans), B (AI plans) and C (plain AI).
- Five decision types: approach, logic, data, edge cases, syntax, with the palindrome example.
- RQ1 to RQ3 each had a hypothesis (A highest on the no-AI test, A still highest after one week, confidence matches better in A).
- The draft talked about building a tool and sending the IRB application.

## Oct 26: Dr. Astorga's and Dr. Gilliland's comments

- Dr. Astorga: "decision" and "condition" were not clear, "planning decisions" was unclear, and the types should be grouped as algorithmic approach, modeling of inputs and testing. His definition: a decision is a choice for one of those categories.
- Dr. Gilliland: plain AI is already studied in [1], so it cannot be a condition. Define "decision" and rework the research questions. He flagged the hypotheses. Quality of AI use came from his comment.
- Changes made: conditions became L1, L2, L3 (ownership levels). The old types stayed as the breakdown inside the three categories. Hypotheses were removed. "Autonomy" became the level of decision control in problem solving. The IRB and tool-building lines were removed.
- Added: the definition of a decision from [16] and [18], with the caution that [16] studied professional developers. The brute-force rule for beginners, matched to Loksa [2]. The palindrome example at L1, L2 and L3.

## Oct 27: Oct 27 version

- Research questions set as RQ1 (no-AI test), RQ2 (one-week retention), RQ3 (quality of AI use), with no hypotheses.
- Experiment framework written: CS1 students, same problems, rotated order, pretest, confidence score.
- Worked examples added for the palindrome, positive/negative/odd/even and movie ticket problems.
- Terms moved to the end. Advisor input listed.

## Nov 1 to Nov 2: working notes

- New problems: payroll (asked for by Dr. Gilliland) and the updated even/odd and movie ticket breakdowns.
- Setup work (headers, input, printing, function layout) is scaffolding and not a decision. Prerequisites are not decisions.
- Both advisors raised that modeling and testing may blend into the algorithmic approach.
- Proposed but not confirmed: a starter template, and early CS1 scope.
- Open: the most important decision, how to score the no-AI test by part, quality of AI use, the order of conditions, zero as even.
