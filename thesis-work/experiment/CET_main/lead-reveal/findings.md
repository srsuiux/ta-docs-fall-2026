The `t7-lead-reveal-iter1.md` experiment gave us some ideas, so it’s all about effective prompting.

When I conducted the experiment, I noticed that the quizzes were being pushed to me, and I just had to choose one of the options. That’s good, but it’s not that reasonable, as it still seems to use less thinking

Instead of choosing from predefined options, I want to study more through hints and explore the topic in greater depth.

So, let’s make this entirely conversation-driven. **Lead** is the user’s conversation with the tutor, and **Reveal** is the part controlled by the AI.

Let’s design a new prompt, which will be `iter2.md`.

**Tested the prompt-2 (iter-2)**

Better interactions and controls than prompt-1. Rather than just clicking on a predefined MCQ answer, the user has to write their answers manually in the chat, which is more engaging and requires more active thinking from the user. The hints are also really helpful in understanding the problem.
